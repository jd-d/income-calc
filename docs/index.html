Fruit Trade Services

Fruit Trade Services Playbook v1.0

A practical menu of what you can offer, how to package it, and how it fits your life. As of November 01, 2025
How to use this playbook

Start with the Quick Start Map to pick a direction (steady vs. hands-on). Then open the detailed service pages in Part C to scope, price, and set simple KPIs. Use the bundles in Part E to combine services that play well together. Print the appendices for intake, KPIs, and scope of work snippets.
